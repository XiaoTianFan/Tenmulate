# Blender grass arena

## Reference and construction brief — 2026-09-05

The third authored venue targets the architectural character of the modern Centre Court at Wimbledon, with neutral **Grass Open Arena** / Tenmulate identity in the product. This is original geometry informed by public references, not a measured replica. Reference names remain here and in development provenance, not in visible signs or runtime mesh names. Removing logos is not a rights-clearance opinion.

The [official seating diagram](https://www.wimbledon.com/pdf/Centre_Court_Plan_2021.pdf) was downloaded and rendered locally for visual inspection. It shows straight longitudinal seating blocks connected by rounded corner fans, a baseline box and nonuniform upper-gallery coverage. It is schematic: it does not publish row slopes or construction dimensions. Owner-supplied empty-bowl photographs are the main source for rake, aisle rhythm, low dark parapets, green seats and the canopy underside. No reference image is shipped.

## Authored construction

The main bowl uses 12 lower rows at 0.29 m rise, 26 main rows at 0.41 m rise and a six-row gallery at 0.43 m rise. All have 0.74 m tread depth and 0.56 m seat spacing. Straight stands retain fixed tangential metre columns and court-normal aisles; circular corner fans absorb the increasing perimeter without twisting the main blocks. The additional gallery stops at the south pavilion and southern corners. Its 14,381 linked seats are an intentional approximation of the roughly 15,000-seat reference, not a copied seat inventory.

Inner bowl half-extents are 12.2 × 22 m, while the 22 × 41 m lawn remains exact. The final outer seating half-extents are 47.11 × 56.91 m; the roof adds a 3 m perimeter overhang. A 61 × 68 m rounded rectangular aperture remains open between two banks of five 77 m-span trusses. The front truss axes are at ±34.7 m with 1.55 m parked pitch; the complete structural cross-sections stay outside the opening. 4.4 m-deep lattices, cambered chords, eight folded skins, travel rails, bogies, motor housings and an opaque grid-panel underside are modeled geometry. The folded fabric uses transmission 0.22 and roughness 0.6; it is not clear alpha glass.

Four sidewall entrances reserve body volumes through the first seating rows. Separate baseline pavilions occupy deliberately omitted seats/terraces. Low perimeter padding, original generic score displays, timber-edged hospitality, player chairs, coolers, umpire ladder, timber net posts and drainage channels complete the court layer. A continuous interior/exterior enclosure and below-bowl foundation prevent sky leaks. Outside is a simplified garden-green facade with window strips, limestone belts and piers, not a modeled real-world precinct.

The original lawn albedo uses longitudinal 0.92 m mowing stripes and restrained baseline wear. Court and runoff share registered world UVs, so there is no texture seam at the playing boundary. Normal/roughness fibre maps repeat six times independently through glTF texture transforms. The four runtime light anchors remain outside roof-cutaway ownership. Blender review lights/cameras are saved in the master but excluded from GLB.

Build with `npm run asset:venue:build -- -Venue grass-center-court`. The existing portable Blender and Meshopt/WebP optimization pipeline produces the packed `.blend`, validated hashed GLB and manifest. [ADR-0011](../decisions/0011-blender-grass-arena.md) preserves the opt-in runtime boundary and procedural default/fallback.

## Delivery status

Built and integrated for local opt-in review. Packed master: **18,764,966 bytes**. Export: `grass-center-court.7d25bddb4c7e.glb`, **6,353,976 bytes / 6.06 MiB**, 74 meshes, 19 materials, 556,164 compressed texture bytes. Actual seat count is **14,381**: 2,153 lower, 10,086 main, 2,072 gallery and 70 pavilion seats. Only the final hash is shipped; the two rejected trial exports are retained outside the repository with local review evidence.

**154 tests / 16 files** and production build pass. Raw glTF validation has **zero errors, zero warnings**, 135 informational notices. Actual decoded tests verify byte/hash budget, regulation anchors, all seat-instance counts and row heights, lawn footprint and continuous UVs, native PBR channels, two five-truss banks clearing the opening, dense folded material, four unblocked entrance body volumes, the rear enclosure, fixed light anchors and absence of reference-event/sponsor names. Manager tests cover native grass materials, cross-venue manifest rejection, lazy loading and cached scene reuse.

The master renders successfully in Cycles, but acceptance evidence uses actual production `TennisScene`. Browser review at 1600 × 1000 and 390 × 844 covers player/corner/sideline/bowl/roof, day/dusk/night, hard/clay/grass/procedural switches and roof shadow resets. The clean warm loop has zero console errors/warnings and no framework overlay. A controlled online reload caches the final grass manifest and GLB; the subsequent offline reload remains authored/ready. A fresh service-worker-blocked context with forced GLB HTTP 503 uses visible procedural grass fallback. The default main application requests zero venue assets. In opt-in main practice, selecting Outdoor Arena · Grass loads authored grass and synchronizes the Grass surface; Start practice reaches the room-safety prompt and Cancel returns to setup. QA did not attest to the user's physical room clearance or start a practice session. See [Stage 16](visual-verification.md#stage-16-third-blender-arena--garden-grass--2026-09-05).

Single-grass day/corner reports 235 draws, 3,404,818 rendered triangles and 10 textures. The existing transmission pass contributes to this work; three loaded venues use 24 textures in the switch study. These are counters, not target-device performance acceptance. Frame-time/memory/cold-load profiling, owner visual sign-off and deployment remain open. Precinct modeling, crowds and operable roof animation are not included. The procedural venue remains the default/failure fallback; existing hard and clay sources and hashes are unchanged.
